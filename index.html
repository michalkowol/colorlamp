Raingel · Bluetooth LE

Lamp control

Connect a lamp or LED strip made for the Raingel app and control colour, modes, music rhythm and schedules from the browser.

Devices

No devices. Turn the lamp on, click Add lamp and pick it from the list.

This browser does not remember paired lamps between visits. In Chrome enable chrome://flags/#enable-web-bluetooth-new-permissions-backend and restart the browser, then the lamp connects by itself when the page opens.

The browser does not always expose the lamp type from the BLE advertisement. If it is missing, pick the type by hand to match the available features (default: Magic, addressable).

Favourites

Selected colour
Presets

Fixed colours on parts of the strip, without animation. The lamp has no per-LED command, so the app paints the whole strip with the top colour, then shrinks the number of controlled LEDs and paints the next segments downwards. LEDs above that number keep their colour.

The shortest bottom segment is 16 LEDs (controller limit). When done, click Restore full length, otherwise modes only run on part of the strip. Any other colour, mode or power command also restores it.

Effect
Colour

The microphone level is sent to the lamp every 150 ms, in the chosen colour or rotating colours. The lamp microphone has no colour setting.

Schedule

No schedules. The lamp stores up to 6 active entries.

TimeActionRepeat

Delay

A countdown after which the lamp runs its timer action (up to 23 h 59 min).

Channel order

If the strip shows wrong colours (for example red lights up green), pick the controller channel order.

Time sync

The time is sent automatically after connecting. Schedules use the lamp clock.
